Goal of our Program

To help encourage every child to participate in every facet of the camp’s activities.

How this helps camps

The advantage that our system can provide for camps is threefold:

  1. Campers come home happier, excited and more fulfilled.

  2. Parents see that their children are happy and excited to go to camp, which provides an easy and free way of promoting the camp.

  3. Most importantly, the “kavana hoelyona” of reaching and affecting every child in camp is more easily attained.

How the camp system works

The camp system can be basically broken down into three components:

  1. Keeping track of the various camp “groups”. The system will keep track of all the bunks, learning classes, etc.

  2. Keeping track of the various camp “activities”. The system will keep track of all the types of things that the head councilor/councilor/learning teacher “assigns” to the camper in order to get points.

  3. Keeping track of all the points accumulated to each of the campers in each group. Additionally, it will keep track of all of the “group” points that are accumulated.

To illustrate the advantage of using the system, we will examine one of the common activities in camp.

Every camp has some sort of bunk competition which always includes “benching”. When it comes time to “bench”, the head councilor awards points to every bunk for their benching performance.  The bunk that performs the best gets the most points while the other bunks get fewer points.
The challenge with this approach is that with time, as one bunk gets ahead, and another bunk falls behind, the bunk(s) that are further behind feel less motivated and it’s gets harder to get them involved.

In our camp system, the way we overcome this challenge, is by the councilor awarding points to each camper for his/her individual performance. This way, even if the camper feels that he/she won’t win in bunk competition, nevertheless it’s worth to perform and receive individual points. All this is only in addition to the fact that each group gets group points.

This is just one of many illustrations of how the program can affect every single camper.
