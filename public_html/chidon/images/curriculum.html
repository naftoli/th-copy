The Curriculum

Student textbooks • $45

The student textbook includes a vast amount of information on each of the 613 Mitzvos. They are grouped by themes, called “units,” and contain sources, halachos, stories, and interesting facts. The student textbook is purchased by the school and used year after year. The first four volumes can be purchased directly from livinglessons.com

Workbooks • $15

The students’ workbook has activities and games to help make sure the information is understood. There are also review questions, which can be assigned as homework, to ensure that the students master the material in preparation for the tests.

Teachers’ guides • $130

The helpful teachers’ guide elaborates on each mitzva, giving teachers additional background along with a focus and goal of each section. It also includes pointers for various ways to introduce the material.

Using the Yahadus Curriculum

While there is a wealth of information in each unit, in school the children will only be responsible for the core information.

If there is additional time, you can move on to supplementary information as you see fit, according to the level of your students.
