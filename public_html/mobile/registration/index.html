Shipping Rates

All children not enrolled in an active Tzivos Hashem Base will need to have their physical items (such as Rank books and Medals) shipped directly. Sadly, as much as we would love to do this for free we simply cannot do so and must charge for shipping.

Please note that this charge is only for children who are not enrolled in an active Tzivos Hashem Base. Children who are enrolled will have their items delivered to them by their Base Commanders.

Shipping rates may vary by location and are subject to group discounts.
