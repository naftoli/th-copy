Chidon Communication Director
Dear Chidon Coordinators, My name is Rabbi Moishy Schapiro and as Chidon Communication Director, my goal is to proactively provide you with all the information you need to know, by making sure the base commander meetings are clear and informative and all the resources and links you need are linked below.
If you have participated in the meetings and reviewed the resources below and still need additional assistance please don't hesitate to contact me at (929) 397-1520 (https://wa.me/19293971520) it will be my pleasure to support you.
Set up Chidon Parents WhatsApp Group
It is imperative that you have a parent WhatsApp group to update your parents on everything going on Chidon related, if you can please add the Chidon Communications Number (https://wa.me/19293971520) to the chat as well. (No need for admin privileges) Additionally, here is a Profile Picture you can use for your WhatsApp if you would like.
Support for your Communication Parents
If a parent has a question about anything Chidon related they should be asking you, in a case where the question is beyond the scope of what you can answer/assist, you can reach out to us regarding the question, but to ensure a smooth operation we ask you please always include the Child's full name and Tzivos Hashem Serial Number - found in the Child’s profile (starting 77#####) - you can search for them here (https://mashpia.com/new/bm/soldiers).
CC Meetings and Fabrengens
There is a monthly Chidon Coordinators meeting in which you will be updated on what to expect during the coming month, review the resources available and guide you through the next stage of the process. To run Chidon successfully in your school you must join these meetings. The Chidon communication director will ONLY answer your questions, and support your requests if you have listened to the meeting. In the case that an exception is needed the recordings of the meetings are available upon request (https://wa.me/19293971520).
