Goal
The goal for this project is to give the children a chance to experience a full rally with the Rebbe, train our children to listen to the Rebbe and understand what the Rebbe is saying.
Purpose
When a child hears the Nossi Hador speak to him it has a very profound impact on his/her life
This applies even if they do not understand what they hear, when they do understand this impact is magnified tremendously. This project will will also give children the tools to sit and watch a rally properly now and iyh to watch a full farbrengen when they are older.
Scope
Connection Point
In the three weeks leading up to Chanukah, Purim Koton, Purim, and Lag B’omer the kids will watch one sicha of the rally each week, an estimated 12 minutes per sicha. The children will follow along as the Rebbe speaks with questions. By the time the rally comes, students will have learned and watched the complete rally. Children who pass the rally quiz will be entered into a raffle.
Real Rallies for Real Soldiers
Create a viewing time in your school for kids who are ready to experience the full rally with the Rebbe
The children have learned and watched all the sichos in school and know the Sichos
The children who join will follow along as the Rebbe speaks with a worksheet/transcript
There will be raffles and nosh by these events
The children who sign up to this program will make a commitment to sit respectfully as one should in front of the general of the entire army, they will not talk or play throughout the entire rally
Each rally club event will be for approx 90 minutes. The schedule includes:
Children davening Mincha with the Rebbe (15 minutes)
12 Pessukim (10 Minutes)
The Rebbe’s 1st Sicha (10 Minutes)
Rabbi JJ Hecht translation (10 Minutes)
The Rebbe’s 2nd Sicha (10 Minutes)
Rabbi JJ Hecht translation (10 Minutes)
The Rebbe’s 3rd Sicha (10 Minutes)
Rabbi JJ Hecht translation (10 Minutes)
Niggun, Tzedoka (5 minutes)
Audience
4th - 8th. Other classes can join if the teacher wishes

Campaign Vehalachta Bidrachav
“Vehalachta Bidrachav” means to go in the way of Hashem, just as Hashem is compassionate so we should also be compassionate etc. At the second Tzivos Hashem rally, the Rebbe tells the children of Tzivos Hashem how they can conquer the world for Hashem: Each week or at least each month they should get together with their general their madrichim/madrichos and learn what it says in chumosh and madroshim about the Maasim Tovim of the Avos and Imahos and Dovid Hamelech and make a group Hachaloto to follow in their ways and win over our Yetzer Hora. The Avos and Imahos are Hashem's best soldiers who truly follow in Hashem's ways and we can be great soldiers by following their example.
What exactly do the chayolim have to do?
There are four distinct missions presently associated with this campaign:
Get together with a squad of friends and their general (madrich/madricha).
Read the Hachayol (PDF) story associated with the middah
Fill out an evaluation sheet
Make a group Hachlatah
Complete the Hachloto

By Rabbi Osher Broh:
Thing big think global:
Tzivos Hashems goal isn’t only to transform a school, or a local community.
Tzivos Hashems mission is global.
Tzivos Hashems goal is to transform the entire world!
We have to reveal, מלא כל הארץ כבודו" “- how the entire world is full of Hashems Glory!
The entire world?
How are we meant to take over the entire world?
Very simple. We took a look at our “Book of Orders” – our Torah.
In Parshas Vayeitzei, Hashem gave Yaakov Avinu the formula of how his descendants, how the future Tzivos Hashem, will succeed in their mission.
Hashem told Yaakov that they should spread out to and affect all four sides of the world:
”[1]ופרצת ימה וקדמה וצפונה ונגבה - “And you will spread out to the west, east, north and south.”
Conquer the world on four fronts.
And that’s exactly the first thing the צבאות ה did.
Four Sides of the World:
At Yetzias Mitzrayim, when Yaakovs descendants formally became the Tzivos Hashem, they camped and travelled in a very specific order.
How did they camp?
The twelve shevatim split into 4 divisions of three. Each of the 4 divisions camped on another side of the Mishkan. Each division camped under their flag or banner.
Four banners on four sides corresponding to the four corners of the world!
The “four sided camping formation” wasn’t just a “good guess” on the Yiddens part. The Yidden were following the exact instructions of Yaakov Avinu.[2]
The instructions of how to fulfill their mission as the Tzivos Hashem.
The four sides don’t only correspond to the four directions of this world.
Hashems army in Shomayim, “camps” in the exact same formation around His Kiseh Hakavod. In fact, each one of the four “Division banners” of the the Yidden in the desert had a symbol on it. A Man, a lion, an ox and an eagle. These were the four images surrounding the Kiseh Hakovod that Yecehzkol saw in his vision.
In addition each banner had another one of the four letters of Hashems name. By camping and conquering in four directions, the Yidden were fighting for Hashem’s name in this world and all the Upper Worlds!
But what does that mean practically? Are should we literally spread out and walk to all four sides of the world?
What do the four sides of the world mean for us?
The Avos and Imahos
Each one of the four sides of the world is connected with one of the Avos and Imahos.
Avraham & Soroh = South
Yitzchok & Rivkah = North
Yaakov & Leah =East
Dovid & Rochel-=West
In order to protect and conquer each one of the four sides of the world, we have to uphold and follow the actions of each of the four Avos and Imahos.
For example, at times the Yetzer Horah will try to attack the south side of the world.
This means that the Yetzer Horah will attack one of the things Avrohom and Sorah stood for.
Gemilus Chassodim.
He’ll try and convince a Yid, not to give Tzedokah, or help someone.
The job of Tzivos Hashem is to learn from Avrohom and Sorah and fight the Yetzer Horah on this front. To make sure we don’t let the “Northern side” get captured.
The same thing applies to all the other sides/Avos and Imahos.
Okay, so we follow the ideals and actions of the Avos and Imahos to protect and conquer the world .
But when are we meant to learn about their ideals and actions?
Are we meant to focus on all the Avos/Imahos all the time?
And where can we find out about their ideals?
Be Prepared – Unite weekly/monthly to plan how to fight.
Every army is split into separate divisions. Each division has a specialized and unique job they are responsible for, to ensure the overall success of the army.
Likewise, Tzivos Hashem, the global army, is split up into divisions.
Four divisions.
Each week or at least each month, the children, soldiers of Tzivos Hashem should get together with their local commander and focus on one of these four divisions. On the ideals and actions of one of the Avos/Imahos.
At this meeting, the soldiers should learn about the ideals and actions of one of the Avos and Imahos and discuss how the Yetzer Horah might try to attack that ideal or Midah. The soldiers have to come up with a plan to thwart this attack and defeat the Yetzer Horah.
This doesn’t mean that the other three sides, the other divisions are ignored and left vulnerable to attack. All divisions must be on guard all the time. Rather that each week or month the soldiers must focus on one of the divisions.
