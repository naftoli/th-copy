Enrollment Tools

In order for any child to be a part of Chidon, they must enroll at Mashpia.com (there are no exceptions), our job is to give the information, tools and encouragement for them to enroll and take part.

During enrollment a Chayol will select what book they are learning, choose their prizes, their sweater (and yarmulka) sizes etc.
After enrollment closes - starting Monday, 26 Tishrei/October 28 and ending Monday, 10 Cheshvan/November 11 - parents and coordinators have the opportunity to review the prizes and sizes they chose, along with confirming the spelling of their future plaque. Once they confirm (or if they don’t bother to confirm at all), everything is final.

Chidon Drive 

  • Chidon Drive is a tremendous tool that we give to families to help them ensure that they don’t have to stress about all the ‘fees’ tied to Chidon.
  • The Chidon drive is something that usually is utilized at the end of the Chidon Limmud close to registration, but for this year we opened it up at the time of enrollment to ensure that families can benefit from this earlier.
  • Each Chayol and family have their own unique link and Flyer that they can share with others to utilize this tool.
  • Every dollar earned goes directly off their families registration costs (not for Additional items like Parent Sweaters, Celebration Boxes or Shipping (When applicable)).

Methods of Use:

  1. As it was intended, to give a Chayols family and friends an opportunity to take part in the Chayols learning, they can provide funds for portions of the goal amount set by the Chayol.

  2. As a tool for parents to utilize if they don’t want to pay all the fees in bulk at the end of the year, instead, they can make smaller payments through this portal, and they can lower the amount needed at the end of the Chidon Limmud - at registration.

KHK

KHK (=Kol Hatorah Kulah) is an additional - add on program for those that are finishing ‘Kol Hatorah Kulah’ this year (i.e. 8th graders who are currently learning the last book of the Yahadus Books).
The program is a Chazarah program, where they will review the Mitzvos they learnt throughout the past 4 years.

In order to take part in the program, the Chayol must have passed Chidon every year on at least the Yediah level since book 1 (4 years ago). They would then have the option to register for the program during enrollment

Note: there is an $18 fee for the program, which includes: the personalized KHK Study Guide, the 4 tests, KHK Final & KHK Plaque

Meetings and Report cards

Before the child starts learning

Review the track they chose and show them their limmud tracker. Explain that the prize they will receive will be based on the time they invest into learning. If they put in the right amount of time they should be able to pass the track. But if they put in the effort and they put in the designated amount of time for their track, even if they don't pass as long as they have filled out their limmud Trackers and given it in each week, they will be awarded the prize they deserve.

After each test

Meet with each chayol personally and review the track they chose, their Limmud Trackers and their report card. Discuss if they put in the amount of time necessary to pass the track they are on. In some cases you may suggest that they go for a lower track. In other cases you may encourage the chayol to go for a higher track. Always give the child the insurance that their prize will reflect the work they are putting in.

After test three

You must have this one-on-one meeting with the child. At times we had a human error entering the marks and at other times there was a tragedy in the family that needs to be considered. You NEVER want the child going home feeling the reward they deserved. You ALWAYS want the child to feel excited to continue and be a Kol Hatorah Kulah Kid.

Before the test three meeting

Use the Limmud Evaluation Report to determine if the child is getting the reward they deserve.

You may want to adjust the passing mark for that child.  You may want to compare to the old format of test marking with cumulative marks. You may want to override the reward they get, by adjusting to a higher or lower reward based on their effort. Please note: All changes must be made before confirming your school's eligibility.

Confirming Eligibility

While you can determine the reward for a child, THAT IS ONLY BEFORE REGISTRATION OPENS. Once registration opens you can not make any changes to their rewards. This is why we have created the Chidon Confirmation Report, your tool to confirm that every child is receiving the correct reward. ONCE you press that button there is no going back. Please note:  Reward registration for your school will not open until you have pressed that button. You must press that button to open Registration.    

By following the steps above and having a yechidus with each child by wednesday the 7th of shevat you still have a few days to make changes. By Sunday the 11th of Shevat at 1pm you must have confirmed eligibility so that your schools reward registration can open on time.         

Chidon Registration

Wait, didn’t we already Register and pay at the beginning of the year?

Many programs start off requiring full registration and payment at the start, but at Chidon we don’t require parents to pay for Rewards and Awards when they don’t know what track their child will pass and possibly may even drop out and not pass any track.


Therefore we only require them to pay as low as  $20 Limmud Enrollment Fee for which they receive their Chidon Kop Card Game, Study Guide, and the 3 Qualifying Tests etc.

Only after the 3 Qualifying Tests when it’s clear what track they passed do we require them to pay the  Reward Registration fee towards their children’s rewards and awards.

To be sure that this was made very clear, the parents were required to check off that they are aware of this concept when they enrolled at the beginning of the year.

Chidon Registration Day

Monday, 12 Shevat/February 10 is Chidon Registration Day - where all Chayolim will register and pay for their rewards and awards.

Chidon Registration Buffer Days

For the following 2 days after Chidon Registration day there will be a time for us to resolve any issue that may arise.

Once Wednesday Night, 14 Shevat/February 12 11:59 PM EST passes it will be too late to resolve issues.

Why is Registration Only for one day? Why so much pressure?

In order that the children have enough time to prepare properly for the test, we have given at least 30 days for learning (besides Yom Tov) for each test. This brings us to Test 3 on Hey Shevat.

Then we have a very small window to ensure that  test 3 can be marked, the marks input into the system and print report cards. Then review the report card privately with each child. Then confirm  the eligibility of each child. Only then can we open reward registration on Monday the 12th of Shevat. Then we provide the two day buffer zone to deal with any issues that may arise. After that on the 14th of Shevat it must close so HQ can ship out the over 120,000 items (of which many are personalized with names and specific sizes) to hundreds of schools and homes around the world and arrive on time for the Regional trips and award ceremonies. For this reason it is imperative that all children are registered on reward registration day.

Chidon Drive

To help parents pay these fees we have created a website called Chidon Drive.

Chidon Drive is very simple to use:

  1. Go to your parent account and click on the Chidon Drive button.
  2. Set up the page for your family.
  3. Send the link to family and friends and ask them to support a movement of over 7000 children who are mastering all 613 Mitzvos in their spare time.

All funds raised on this site go towards helping parents with the reward registration. Any money raised by a child will be deducted from their registration fee.

In a case that a parent would like to move funds from one child to another. We do not have programming in place to do so. But parents or base commanders can reach out to Chidon Drive Support and provide the name and serial number of the child who raised money, the name and serial number of the child who they want it to go to, and the amount to transfer and this will be done from the back end by our staff. After they have made the transfer the Chidon Drive Support will notify the person who requested that it's been taken care of.

Please transfer $xxx from First Name > Last Name > Serial Number.

Please Distribute $xxx to First  Name > Last Name > Serial Number and $xxx to First  Name > Last Name > Serial Number.

The last time to transfer funds is before the Chidon drive closes at 1pm EST on Sunday 11th Shevat. Chidon drive must close before reward registration is open in order to ensure that all funds that were raised will be available by reward  registration.

Please encourage your parents to start fundraising as soon as possible in order to have ample time to raise the funds they need before the Chidon drive closes.

Chidon Drive closes on Sunday, 11 Shevat/February 9 at 1pm EST.

Prepaying for Personalized Prizes

At the beginning of the Year (by limmud enrollment) we had everyone choose 75 credits worth of prizes. Anyone that chose a prize that was personalized had to pre-pay. It was very clear that in the case that your child is not eligible for prizes the money would not be refundable.

Please note: We reached out to each of those parents personally both by email and WhatsApp several times offering them to pay. For those who did not respond we made their prize without personalization.

We are providing you with a list of parents' whose personalization payment did not go through. If any of them are in your base please notify them that because they were not yet charged for their personalized prize they will be charged at registration.

After registration is closed we will have their prize personalized.

 

Prepaying for reward Registration

There were some parents (by Chidon enrollment) who opted to pre-pay for reward registration. The reason parents would prepay is because it allows parents to pay in 4 installments over 4 months.

Refunds

As promised this money is totally refundable.

In the case that a parent overpaid, as their child passed a lower track then what they prepaid for they can:

  • Request a refund of the difference between what they paid and the cost of the track their child passed.
  • Choose to use this money towards parents and grandparents sweaters and shipping.
  • Donate the funds to Chidon.

Please note as mentioned before that the money that they prepaid for their personalized prizes are not refundable.

So if a parent prepaid $200 of which $75 was for a personalized prize there will be $125 available to refund. If they prepaid $100 of which $75 was for a personalized prize, there will be $25 available to refund.

In the case that the parents prepaid and their child did not pass any track and therefore there is no link for that child to register,  the parents will be able to request a refund on their mashpia.com account after registration closes.

Ultimate Chidon Experience

Qualifications

This trip is for children in 8th grade who have learned for the Chidon for all five years and have learned all 613 Mitzvos.

For this year ONLY we will be allowing children who learned for at least 4 out of the 5 years. In previous years they had to pass at least on the Yediah level and for this year they would have to pass on the havona level.

Please note: please review which children in your school are eligible. If there is a child that should be eligible and is not, please contact Chidon communications to adjust that.

Children in 8th grade that do not qualify for the Ultimate Chidon Experience and passed Havona, do not join the Ultimate Chidon Experience. They go together with their school on their regional trip.

Exceptions 

In the case that a child did pass on the Yediah track for 3 years in the past and only passed on the Yediah track this year, and so they are not eligible for the Ultimate Chidon Experience. But you believe based on their effort they should be eligible. You can use the Limmud Evaluation Report to change their reward for this year to Havona. That will make them eligible for the Ultimate Chidon Experience.

Choice of Reward

Even if a child is eligible for the Ultimate Chidon Experience. But the parents do not want to sign them up for this trip. By reward registration, they can choose to go on their Regional Trip.

No prizes

Any child who goes on the ultimate Chidon experience does not receive prizes. If they choose to go on the Regional trips they do get prizes.

Contracts 

During the Reward Registration, they will have to check off that they signed the Ultimate Chidon Experience contract.

This contract is to ensure that we will be able to ensure proper safety and behavior during the experience.

In the case a child does not feel they can live up to the standards they should not sign up. If they do and sign up and don't live up to the standards clearly set in the contract they will be asked to leave and their parents will not be refunded.

Please note this is not a threat. In previous years this has been enforced and children have been dismissed from the program.

Home / Host

If a chayol is signing up for the Ultimate Chidon Experience, during reward registration they will be asked to fill out where they are staying. If their host isn’t confirmed yet, they can input the address of 770 Eastern Parkway as a placeholder; they must edit it once their host is confirmed.

Walking groups

There will not be official walking groups. As most of the children want to walk home on their own. Even most of the children that are coming in front out of town tend to find family and friends to walk home with.

On Thursday night and Motzei Shabbos you can sign up for the bus to be driven to your host. IF YOU SIGN UP FOR THE BUS YOU MUST GO ON THE BUS.

Please note:

  1. Each time the children need to get to the program they need to get there on their own. This includes: Wednesday Night. Thursday, Friday, Shabbos and Sunday morning.
  2. On Wednesday Night after orientation and Friday afternoon and Sunday after the banquet there will be no bus transportation. They must find their own way home.
  3. When you sign up for the bus please note that they will get home up to one hour later than by walking as the bus needs to make many stops all around crown heights.
  4. We encourage girls to walk home in groups. Unofficial walking groups can be made.

Chidon Final

"כל הלומד ואינו חוזר כאילו זורע ואינו קוצר"

This is the most important month to really study hard. Learning the book in three parts will help the chayolim pass the test. It's learning for the final that will help them retain this information. Please do all you can to encourage them to give all they can this month.

ONLY children who have registered for rewards and awards can take the final.

Final Dates:

  • Book 1-5 Final (all tracks, including online Iyun): Monday, 3 Adar/March 3
  • KHK Online Final: Thursday, 6 Adar/March 6
  • Marks due NO EXCEPTIONS:  Thursday, 6 Adar/March 6.
  • At 11:59PM (EST) on Thursday, 6 Adar/March 6 all awards will be final and we will not be able to change any marks/awards NO MATTER THE REASON

Test Links:

  • English Tests
  • Yiddish Tests
  • Answer Key
  • If you will be giving the online finals (Iyun or KHK) in school Please reach out to Chidon Communications to get the links.  

⛔ Under no circumstances should any child receive their final back.⛔

We, at HQ, take this very seriously, and if any child walks out of the room with a test or receives the actual test back, there will be serious repercussions

Each Chayol can ONLY take the final for the tracks they passed.

  • A child who passed Yesod can ONLY take the Yesod section of the final
  • A child who passed Yediah can ONLY take the Yesod and Yediah sections of the final
  • A child who passed Havonah can ONLY take the Yesod, Yediah and Havonah sections of the final
  • ONLY a child who has passed Iyun can take the online Iyun final

There are a total of 60 questions on the printed final to be done in school:

  • 20 Yesod Questions
  • 20 Yediah Questions
  • 20 Havana Questions
  • If a child will not be in school on the day of the test please fill out this form.

The Iyun part of the final is online, it includes:

  • 20 Iyun Questions
  • One tie-breaker question The format of the question will be "Write as many mitzvos as you can that apply..."
  • The online final can be done in school on a device that can access the online test, but it must be under the supervision of the coordinator. Please reach out to Chidon Communications to get the links.
  • If a school can not facilitate the online part of the test, the chayol can take that part at home after school over zoom. Instruction Document Link to send to parent.

Awards are determined based solely on this test. Here’s how they are earned:

  • Chidon Certificate: 80% on Yesod (16/20 on the first 20 questions)
  • Chidon Plaque: 80% on Yediah (16/20 on both the first & second 20 questions)
  • Chidon Plaque+Medal: 80% on Havonah (16/20 on the first, second & third 20 questions)
  • Chidon Plaque+Medal+Blue Trophy: 80% on Iyun (16/20 on the first, second, third & last 20 questions)
  • Bronze, Silver, and Gold Trophies: Top 3 marks in each grade
  • To lower the passing mark to a 70% on Yesod, Yediah, or Havona (for a specific child, class, or your entire school): https://mashpia.com/chidonTests/settings.php

Please Note: for Iyun you CANNOT lower the passing mark.

  • To see the passing mark on Yesod, Yediah, or Havona (for a specific child, class, or your entire school): https://mashpia.com/chidonTests/reports/settings_report.html 

KHK Online Final:

  • 200 Who/Where/When/Punishment questions
  • 15 optional tiebreaker Makor questions (from Books 4 & 5) that will help determine the KHK trophy winners. These questions will be multiple choice.
  • 1 optional ultimate tiebreaker question which will be used if there is still a tie after the Makor questions. The format of the question will be "Write as many mitzvos as you can that apply..."
  • Awards - KHK Plaque: 70% average on the 3 regular book 5 tests, the 4 KHK tests & on the KHK Final
