Goal

Tehillim on Shabbos Mevorchim was initiated by the Frierdiker Rebbe’s chassidim to fulfill his directive of saying the entire Tehillim with the community. “It is crucial for you, for your children and your children's children.” (Hayom Yom, 25 Shvat).

What is the structure of the Shabbos Mevorchim Tehillim Campaign?

The Shabbos Mevorchim Tehillim Campaign is a regimen of increasing kapitelach and minutes over the course of the 95 months that a chayol serves in Tzivos Hashem. Chayolim can start with 1 kapitel for 10 minutes in their first month of Pre-1-A and can finish with 150 kapitlach in 2 hours at the end of 8th grade.

Commanders (teachers and parents) encourage chayolim to complete their quotas every month and increase their quotas when they are able, so that they will reach the goal of saying the whole Tehilim on Shabbos Mevorchim by the end of their TH army service (bar or bas mitzva).

How do chayolim stay motivated to reach and increase their quotas?

The WWTC (Worldwide Tehillim Club) combines every chayol’s Tehillim quota in the entire Tzivos Hashem brigade to establish a goal for every Shabbos Mevorchim. The WWTC Report from the Battlefront, published monthly in Hachayol, shares the results of whether or not we have reached this brigade-wide goal; it also highlights bases and platoons who have excelled in number of kapitlach, time spent, and participation. Individual chayolim who have otherwise excelled in the campaign will also be featured monthly.

Additional recognition is given to chayolim when they receive medals for completing their missions. After their first 5 missions ( five months of reaching their quota), they receive a white medal, followed by red (6 more missions; 11 completed months), orange (18), yellow (26), green (35), blue (45), purple (56), brown (68), grey (81), and, finally, black (95), for reaching their quota of Tehillim for every single month they were enrolled in Tzivos Hashem! They also receive miles for doing every mission, which can be redeemed for prizes in their base store and used for purchasing raffle tickets in the yearly auction.

How does the Tehillim training process work and how can a commander help a chayol grow?

There are five skill levels per grade, called ladders, that outline a system of increasing quotas over the course of these 95 months. The quotas are calibrated to adapt to each individual child’s skill (how many minutes it takes to say one kapitel, on average), so that each chayol can succeed. The ladders system helps chayolim accustom themselves to saying Tehillim in increasing amounts in an enduring manner within a 95-level course over eight years of their chinuch.

Base commanders should print out a Teacher Letter (https://www.dropbox.com/s/n2g6yfsob1sijox/Shabbos%20Mevorchim%20Teacher%20Letter.pdf?dl=0) for every chayol according to grade and have the teachers test each chayol for their speed in kriah, so they can choose the correct ladder/quota. After teachers have helped with the ladder-choosing process, print his Parent Letter (https://www.dropbox.com/s/5sy5ee6oklh4ne0/Shabbos%20Mevorchim%20Parent%20Letter.pdf?dl=0) and fill it out to be sent home.

By Rabbi Osher Broh:

Thing big think global:

Tzivos Hashems goal isn’t only to transform a school, or a local community.

Tzivos Hashems mission is global.

Tzivos Hashems goal is to transform the entire world!

We have to reveal, מלא כל הארץ כבודו" “- how the entire world is full of Hashems Glory!

The entire world?

How are we meant to take over the entire world?

Very simple. We took a look at our “Book of Orders” – our Torah.

In Parshas Vayeitzei, Hashem gave Yaakov Avinu the formula of how his descendants, how the future Tzivos Hashem, will succeed in their mission.

Hashem told Yaakov that they should spread out to and affect all four sides of the world:

”[1]ופרצת ימה וקדמה וצפונה ונגבה - “And you will spread out to the west, east, north and south.”

Conquer the world on four fronts.

And that’s exactly the first thing the צבאות ה did.

Four Sides of the World:

At Yetzias Mitzrayim, when Yaakovs descendants formally became the Tzivos Hashem, they camped and travelled in a very specific order.

How did they camp?

The twelve shevatim split into 4 divisions of three. Each of the 4 divisions camped on another side of the Mishkan. Each division camped under their flag or banner.

Four banners on four sides corresponding to the four corners of the world!

The “four sided camping formation” wasn’t just a “good guess” on the Yiddens part. The Yidden were following the exact instructions of Yaakov Avinu.[2]

The instructions of how to fulfill their mission as the Tzivos Hashem.

The four sides don’t only correspond to the four directions of this world.

Hashems army in Shomayim, “camps” in the exact same formation around His Kiseh Hakavod. In fact, each one of the four “Division banners” of the the Yidden in the desert had a symbol on it. A Man, a lion, an ox and an eagle. These were the four images surrounding the Kiseh Hakovod that Yecehzkol saw in his vision.

In addition each banner had another one of the four letters of Hashems name. By camping and conquering in four directions, the Yidden were fighting for Hashem’s name in this world and all the Upper Worlds!

But what does that mean practically? Are should we literally spread out and walk to all four sides of the world?

What do the four sides of the world mean for us?

The Avos and Imahos

Each one of the four sides of the world is connected with one of the Avos and Imahos.

Avraham & Soroh = South

Yitzchok & Rivkah = North

Yaakov & Leah =East

Dovid & Rochel-=West

In order to protect and conquer each one of the four sides of the world, we have to uphold and follow the actions of each of the four Avos and Imahos.

For example, at times the Yetzer Horah will try to attack the south side of the world.

This means that the Yetzer Horah will attack one of the things Avrohom and Sorah stood for.

Gemilus Chassodim.

He’ll try and convince a Yid, not to give Tzedokah, or help someone.

The job of Tzivos Hashem is to learn from Avrohom and Sorah and fight the Yetzer Horah on this front. To make sure we don’t let the “Northern side” get captured.

The same thing applies to all the other sides/Avos and Imahos.

Okay, so we follow the ideals and actions of the Avos and Imahos to protect and conquer the world .

But when are we meant to learn about their ideals and actions?

Are we meant to focus on all the Avos/Imahos all the time?

And where can we find out about their ideals?

Be Prepared – Unite weekly/monthly to plan how to fight.

Every army is split into separate divisions. Each division has a specialized and unique job they are responsible for, to ensure the overall success of the army.

Likewise, Tzivos Hashem, the global army, is split up into divisions.

Four divisions.

Each week or at least each month, the children, soldiers of Tzivos Hashem should get together with their local commander and focus on one of these four divisions. On the ideals and actions of one of the Avos/Imahos.

At this meeting, the soldiers should learn about the ideals and actions of one of the Avos and Imahos and discuss how the Yetzer Horah might try to attack that ideal or Midah. The soldiers have to come up with a plan to thwart this attack and defeat the Yetzer Horah.

This doesn’t mean that the other three sides, the other divisions are ignored and left vulnerable to attack. All divisions must be on guard all the time. Rather that each week or month the soldiers must focus on one of the divisions.

Chidon Drive is a tremendous tool that we give to families to help them ensure that they don’t have to stress about all the ‘fees’ tied to Chidon.

As it was intended, to give a Chayols family and friends an opportunity to take part in the Chayols learning, they can provide funds for portions of the goal amount set by the Chayol.

Meetings and Report cards

Before the child starts learning

Review the track they chose and show them their limmud tracker. Explain that the prize they will receive will be based on the time they invest into learning. If they put in the right amount of time they should be able to pass the track. But if they put in the effort and they put in the designated amount of time for their track, even if they don't pass as long as they have filled out their limmud Trackers and given it in each week, they will be awarded the prize they deserve.

After each test

Meet with each chayol personally and review the track they chose, their Limmud Trackers and their report card. Discuss if they put in the amount of time necessary to pass the track they are on. In some cases you may suggest that they go for a lower track. In other cases you may encourage the chayol to go for a higher track. Always give the child the insurance that their prize will reflect the work they are putting in.

After test three

You must have this one-on-one meeting with the child. At times we had a human error entering the marks and at other times there was a tragedy in the family that needs to be considered. You NEVER want the child going home feeling the reward they deserved. You ALWAYS want the child to feel excited to continue and be a Kol Hatorah Kulah Kid.

Before the test three meeting

Use the Limmud Evaluation Report (https://mashpia.com/chidonOld/newReports/limudEval.html) to determine if the child is getting the reward they deserve.

You may want to adjust the passing mark for that child. You may want to compare to the old format of test marking with cumulative marks. You may want to override the reward they get, by adjusting to a higher or lower reward based on their effort. Please note: All changes must be made before confirming your school's eligibility.

Chidon Registration

Wait, didn’t we already Register and pay at the beginning of the year?

Many programs start off requiring full registration and payment at the start, but at Chidon we don’t require parents to pay for Rewards and Awards when they don’t know what track their child will pass and possibly may even drop out and not pass any track.

Therefore we only require them to pay as low as $20 Limmud Enrollment Fee for which they receive their Chidon Kop Card Game, Study Guide, and the 3 Qualifying Tests etc.

Only after the 3 Qualifying Tests when it’s clear what track they passed do we require them to pay the Reward Registration fee towards their children’s rewards and awards.

To be sure that this was made very clear, the parents were required to check off that they are aware of this concept when they enrolled at the beginning of the year.

Chidon Registration Day

Monday, 12 Shevat/February 10 is Chidon Registration Day - where all Chayolim will register and pay for their rewards and awards.

Chidon Registration Buffer Days

For the following 2 days after Chidon Registration day there will be a time for us to resolve any issue that may arise.

Once Wednesday Night, 14 Shevat/February 12 11:59 PM EST passes it will be too late to resolve issues.

Why is Registration Only for one day? Why so much pressure?

In order that the children have enough time to prepare properly for the test, we have given at least 30 days for learning (besides Yom Tov) for each test. This brings us to Test 3 on Hey Shevat.

Then we have a very small window to ensure that test 3 can be marked, the marks input into the system and print report cards. Then review the report card privately with each child. Then confirm the eligibility of each child. Only then can we open reward registration on Monday the 12th of Shevat. Then we provide the two day buffer zone to deal with any issues that may arise. After that on the 14th of Shevat it must close so HQ can ship out the over 120,000 items (of which many are personalized with names and specific sizes) to hundreds of schools and homes around the world and arrive on time for the Regional trips and award ceremonies. For this reason it is imperative that all children are registered on reward registration day.

Ultimate Chidon Experience

Qualifications

This trip is for children in 8th grade who have learned for the Chidon for all five years and have learned all 613 Mitzvos.

For this year ONLY we will be allowing children who learned for at least 4 out of the 5 years. In previous years they had to pass at least on the Yediah level and for this year they would have to pass on the havona level.

Please note: please review which children in your school are eligible. If there is a child that should be eligible and is not, please contact Chidon communications to adjust that.

Children in 8th grade that do not qualify for the Ultimate Chidon Experience and passed Havona, do not join the Ultimate Chidon Experience. They go together with their school on their regional trip.

Exceptions

In the case that a child did pass on the Yediah track for 3 years in the past and only passed on the Yediah track this year, and so they are not eligible for the Ultimate Chidon Experience. But you believe based on their effort they should be eligible. You can use the Limmud Evaluation Report to change their reward for this year to Havona. That will make them eligible for the Ultimate Chidon Experience.

Choice of Reward

Even if a child is eligible for the Ultimate Chidon Experience. But the parents do not want to sign them up for this trip. By reward registration, they can choose to go on their Regional Trip.

No prizes

Any child who goes on the ultimate Chidon experience does not receive prizes. If they choose to go on the Regional trips they do get prizes.

Contracts

During the Reward Registration, they will have to check off that they signed the Ultimate Chidon Experience contract.

This contract is to ensure that we will be able to ensure proper safety and behavior during the experience.

In the case a child does not feel they can live up to the standards they should not sign up. If they do and sign up and don't live up to the standards clearly set in the contract they will be asked to leave and their parents will not be refunded.

Please note this is not a threat. In previous years this has been enforced and children have been dismissed from the program.
