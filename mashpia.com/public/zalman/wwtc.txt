Campaign WWTC:
Goal
Tehillim on Shabbos Mevorchim was initiated by the Frierdiker Rebbe’s chassidim to fulfill his directive of saying the entire Tehillim with the community. “It is crucial for you, for your children and your children's children.” (Hayom Yom, 25 Shvat).
What is the structure of the Shabbos Mevorchim Tehillim Campaign?
The Shabbos Mevorchim Tehillim Campaign is a regimen of increasing kapitelach and minutes over the course of the 95 months that a chayol serves in Tzivos Hashem. Chayolim can start with 1 kapitel for 10 minutes in their first month of Pre-1-A and can finish with 150 kapitlach in 2 hours at the end of 8th grade.
Commanders (teachers and parents) encourage chayolim to complete their quotas every month and increase their quotas when they are able, so that they will reach the goal of saying the whole Tehilim on Shabbos Mevorchim by the end of their TH army service (bar or bas mitzva).

How do chayolim stay motivated to reach and increase their quotas?
The WWTC (Worldwide Tehillim Club) combines every chayol’s Tehillim quota in the entire Tzivos Hashem brigade to establish a goal for every Shabbos Mevorchim. The WWTC Report from the Battlefront, published monthly in Hachayol, shares the results of whether or not we have reached this brigade-wide goal; it also highlights bases and platoons who have excelled in number of kapitlach, time spent, and participation. Individual chayolim who have otherwise excelled in the campaign will also be featured monthly.
Additional recognition is given to chayolim when they receive medals for completing their missions. After their first 5 missions ( five months of reaching their quota), they receive a white medal, followed by red (6 more missions; 11 completed months), orange (18), yellow (26), green (35), blue (45), purple (56), brown (68), grey (81), and, finally, black (95), for reaching their quota of Tehillim for every single month they were enrolled in Tzivos Hashem! They also receive miles for doing every mission, which can be redeemed for prizes in their base store and used for purchasing raffle tickets in the yearly auction.
How does the Tehillim training process work and how can a commander help a chayol grow?
There are five skill levels per grade, called ladders, that outline a system of increasing quotas over the course of these 95 months. The quotas are calibrated to adapt to each individual child’s skill (how many minutes it takes to say one kapitel, on average), so that each chayol can succeed. The ladders system helps chayolim accustom themselves to saying Tehillim in increasing amounts in an enduring manner within a 95-level course over eight years of their chinuch.
Base commanders should print out a Teacher Letter (https://www.dropbox.com/s/n2g6yfsob1sijox/Shabbos%20Mevorchim%20Teacher%20Letter.pdf?dl=0) for every chayol according to grade and have the teachers test each chayol for their speed in kriah, so they can choose the correct ladder/quota. After teachers have helped with the ladder-choosing process, print his Parent Letter (https://www.dropbox.com/s/5sy5ee6oklh4ne0/Shabbos%20Mevorchim%20Parent%20Letter.pdf?dl=0) and fill it out to be sent home.
Chayolim should be encouraged to challenge themselves when choosing their ladder, but not to take on a ladder that is too difficult for them to complete their quota and succeed. When a ladder is chosen and entered on the database, the amount of Tehillim/time will appear on their mission sheet.
How do commanders enter the quota for the mission sheets, mobile site or app?
Teachers or base commanders can set the chayol’s new ladder level by going to mashpia.com> Campaigns> Tehillim> Change tehillim ladder/quota.
Parents can also set the chayol’s ladder by logging into their account on mashpia.com/mobile or on the app, then clicking that child’s account, then the tab for Missios> Personalize missions> Shabbos Mevorchim Tehillim. (Click here (https://www.dropbox.com/s/1nj8mkkvqoxeydc/Personalize%20Ladder%20Page%20from%20Hachayol%20285%20Toldos.pdf?dl=0) to print these instructions for parents.) Note that ladder-changing will be available every month for just one week, beginning on the Sunday before Shabbos Mevorchim.
How can I notify Chayolim of their quota?
Print the NEW Tehillim Quota Cards (http://mashpia.com/quota_cards.php) that personalizes each chayol’s quota as a reminder before Shabbos Mevorchim. After Shabbos Mevorchim, they should bring them back signed and filled in with how much they completed. You can then enter the amounts they said on your account under Campaigns>tehillim>mark Shabbos Mevorchim Tehillim. Alternatively, parents can enter their children’s completed amounts on their mobile site missions. However it will not help if they enter them on their paper missions which are only due the following Thursday which is already after the Shabbos Mevorchim Data Entry Deadline. Therefore, it is important for them to give them these quota cards so they can bring them back in the Monday after Shabbos Mevorchim.
How and when are quotas counted?
Base commanders should remind teachers to ask chayolim to fill in their Shabbos Mevorchim Tehillim missions. Missions can be entered on parents’ mobile site or app for immediate processing. Base commanders can manually enter the data using their mashpia.com account under Campaigns> Tehillim> Mark Shabbos Mevorchim Tehillim.
Teachers can enter completed missions by logging onto their teacher account on mashpia.com (teacher username/pw can be accessed on the base commander’s account under Base management> Teacher logins). For teachers to enter data, they need to ensure that their class is signed up to the Tehillim Campaign by clicking Customize Tasks/Columns and then choosing the correct date.
All data must be entered by the due date on Tuesday following Shabbos Mevorchim in order to be featured on the WWTC Report. It is important to ensure that chayolim submit their Shabbos Mevorchim Tehillim mission sheets on time. If a commander is manually entering the missions, chayolim must become accustomed to completing the mission and bringing it back to school on Monday, for data entry to be completed on Wednesday. All Shabbos Mevorchim due dates are listed in the BC Calendar.
How do commanders encourage their chayolim?
Commanders help the chayolim choose and increase their quotas according to their age and skill, provide them with the ideal environment for performance, encourage them to fill out their Shabbos Mevorchim mission sheets, and celebrate effort and successes. You can view each chayol’s quota under Campaigns > Tehillim > Check your Tehillim quotas.
What is a way to check the children’s summer quota to let them know in advance?
The quotas remain the same for all 3 summer Shabbos Mevorchim months- Tammuz, Av, & Elul.
