# Other Features

---

## Mission Marathon (`/raffles/`)

**Access:** BC, HQ (requires Chayolei module)

**Purpose:** Manage the Mission Marathon raffle program. Soldiers earn raffle entries by completing missions, and winners are drawn at the end of the marathon period.

---

## Yearly Gift (`/yearly_prize/reports/eligible_students.php`)

**Access:** BC, HQ (requires Chayolei module)

**Purpose:** View which soldiers are eligible for the annual yearly gift/prize based on their mission completion throughout the year.

The report shows students who have met the eligibility criteria (typically a minimum number of missions completed) and are therefore entitled to receive the year-end prize.

---

## Manuals (`/manuals`)

**Access:** BC, HQ (requires Chayolei module)

**Purpose:** Access program manuals, guides, and instructional documents for running the Tzivos Hashem program at your base.

Includes guides for:
- Running medal ceremonies
- Chidon competition management
- Mission sheet distribution
- And other program activities

---

## Support (`/helpdesk/?p=open`)

**Access:** All users

**Purpose:** Open a support ticket with the Tzivos Hashem helpdesk.

### How to Use

1. Click **Support** in the main menu
2. Fill in your support request
3. Submit — the HQ support team will respond

Use this for:
- Technical issues with the platform
- Questions about program features
- Requesting changes or additions

---

## HQ Links (`/admin.php?oldsite=1`)

**Access:** HQ only

**Purpose:** Access the legacy HQ administrative dashboard. This is the old admin interface, still available for features not yet migrated to the new system.
