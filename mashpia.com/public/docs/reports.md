# Reports

The Reports section provides a wide variety of printable and downloadable reports about your soldiers, registrations, miles, and more.

> **Tip:** Most reports are designed for printing. Use **Chrome or Firefox** for best results. Many include page breaks between schools and export options.

---

## Registered Report (`/registered_report.php`)

**Access:** BC, HQ

**Purpose:** View all soldiers who have completed their annual Tzivos Hashem registration for a selected year.

### What You See

Per school, per student:
- Family ID
- Grade
- Student name
- Serial number
- TH Start Date (when they first joined the program)
- Registration Date (for the selected year)

Also shows **totals by grade and school**, and a grand total at the bottom.

### How to Use

1. Select the **year** from the dropdown (defaults to current year; up to 5 years back available)
2. The report refreshes automatically
3. Print (page breaks between schools)

## Not Yet Registered Report (`/non_registered_report.php`)

**Access:** BC, HQ

**Purpose:** Find all soldiers who have **not yet registered** for the current year, to follow up with their families.

### What You See

Per school, per student:
- Grade, student name, serial number
- Start date (when they joined TH)
- Date of birth
- Parent email, home phone, work phone, mobile phone

Also shows totals per grade/school and a grand total.

### Actions

- **Print** — includes page breaks per school
- **Export to CSV** — downloads a spreadsheet for mail-merge or follow-up

> **CSV works best** in Chrome/Firefox with Excel 2016+. Not compatible with Internet Explorer or Edge.

## Stickers — Total Stickers Earned (`/stickers_report.php`)

**Access:** BC

**Purpose:** See each soldier's mission completion progress and where they stand toward earning the next medal color for selected missions.

### What You See

- Per class, per soldier: student name with rank prefix
- One column per selected mission showing: **X/Y to [medal color]**
  - Example: "3/5 to Red" means the soldier has 3 stickers and needs 5 to earn a Red medal
- Mission sticker images appear as column headers

### How to Use

1. **Select missions** to include — checkboxes for each mission
   - Use **Check All** / **Uncheck All** for quick selection
2. **Select classes** to include (single-school BCs only)
3. Click Generate / Submit
4. Print in **landscape orientation** (recommended for wide tables)

## Birthday Cards (`/school_birthdays.php`)

**Access:** BC

**Purpose:** Generate printable birthday certificates for soldiers, filtered by Hebrew Parsha (weekly Torah portion) or date range.

### How to Use

1. **Select school(s)** — multi-select if you manage multiple schools
2. **Choose filter method:**
   - **By Parsha** — select one or more Torah portions from the list
   - **By Date Range** — use the Hebrew date picker to set a start and end date
3. **Select gender:** Boys or Girls
4. Review your selections in the summary panel
5. Click **Generate Birthday Certificates**
6. A printable page opens — print and distribute

> At least one school and one Parsha (or date range) must be selected before you can generate.
