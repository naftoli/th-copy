The tests will be provided by Tzivos Hashem and will be administered and marked by each school. The tests should be taken on the designated dates, unless the schools decides otherwise.

All Chayolim who get a passing mark (combined from the two tests) may be eligible to join their fellow Chayolim from around the world for the Chidon Shabbaton! It�s the schools decision what is considered a passing mark.

Up to two Chayolim who scored the highest on the pre-Chidon tests from each grade will represent their school at the Chidon in Crown Heights, where they will take a final written test on the entire book to select the top winners. (All others are not responsible to learn the rest of the book).
