Terms

I authorize my child's chaperone to sign the trip waivers on my behalf if needed. I understand that Chidon will not be responsible for any injury that may occur.

I am aware that by participating in the Chidon I consent to my child's name and picture being used in any media produced by Tzivos Hashem, for publicity purposes or otherwise.

Please read with your child and initial: I will abide by all rules established by Chidon headquarters. Any behavior deemed inappropriate by staff or a departure from an activity without the consent of your counselor and safety officer is terms for dismissal from the Program. The Program fee will not be refunded.

The Chidon Program is a major undertaking, and requires extensive coordination to ensure everyone's safety at all times. For this reason, changes to information submitted after enrollment will incur additional charges:

From Feb 23, changes can be made through your account at an additional charge of $50.

On Wednesday the day before the Program begins, changes can be made between 12:00 and 5:00pm in the lobby of the Jewish children's Museum at a cost of $100.

Any changes we are informed about during the actual Program will incur a $150 charge and must be submitted at the information desk.
