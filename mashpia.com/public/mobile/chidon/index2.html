SHABBATON ENROLLMENT

Chidon HQ will have counselors looking after your children during the program each day. Chaperones are responsible for bringing the children to and from the programs, as well as looking after them once the program ends. Chaperones are also responsible to be on call and take care of the children in the event that an emergency situation arises.

Please understand that due to the large number of students that will be attending the Shabbaton, Chidon HQ will not be responsible for student's arrangements. Each school/family is responsible for arranging their own accommodations and transportation.
