REGISTRATION

Thanks for adding your child(ren) and welcome to the Registration Process!

You will be able to register your newly addes child(ren) as well as any other children that you have with us in one seamless process.

After clicking on "Get Started", if you have any additional children with us, you will need to confirm the information on file for each of them, as well as indicate whether you are registering them now or at a future date.

We look forward to having your child(ren) for another wonderful year!
