KHK Chidon Photo

Instructions to Take a Good Picture:

(Please remember that you will be asked to retake the picture if it doesn’t fulfill these requirements.)
The image should be taken vertically with the chayol in the middle (not cropped arms or top of head - we should see wall on all sides besides for the bottom).
The image should be taken straight on, not from a top or bottom angle.
The image should show your child from the waist up with their arms down.
Your child should be smiling and looking at the camera.
The image should be focused and not blurry.
The photo should be taken in a well-lit area with no face shadows or dark spots.
Your child should be dressed neatly, wearing the Chidon Sweater.
Boys should be wearing a Chidon Yarmulke with the logo facing forward.
Your child should look like a Tzivos Hashem soldier i.e. Tznius, a straight Chidon Yarmulke.
